About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electronic Engineering at Sogang University, advised on efficient deep learning. I was a visiting researcher at Carnegie Mellon University (2024–2025) through the Short Term Scholarship program in Software and Societal Systems.

My research focuses on designing models that are not only accurate but efficient enough for real-world deployment — including lightweight transformer architectures, sparse attention mechanisms, token pruning, and vision-language model compression.

Research Interests

  • Lightweight Architecture: Transformer-based efficient architectures for vision tasks
  • Efficient Inference: Sparse attention, token pruning, parameter-efficient tuning, weight pruning
  • Generative Models: Video diffusion, diffusion multimodal language models
